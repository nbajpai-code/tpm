# RACI Matrix: [Program Name]

> **RACI Framework Definition:**
> *   **R — Responsible**: The person/team who performs the work to complete the task. (Can be multiple).
> *   **A — Accountable**: The person with final decision-making power and ownership. (Exactly **one** person per row).
> *   **C — Consulted**: People whose input is sought before or during the task (two-way communication).
> *   **I — Informed**: People who are kept up-to-date on progress or outcomes (one-way communication).

## 🔍 Key Rules for RACI Alignment

1.  **Exactly one 'A' per row**: If there are multiple Accountables, decisions stall. If there are zero, no one owns the outcome.
2.  **Minimize the number of 'C's**: Too many Consulted roles leads to analysis paralysis and slows down execution.
3.  **Role vs. Name**: Fill out the RACI matrix using specific roles or actual names (e.g., "John Doe" or "DevOps Engineer") to avoid ambiguity.
4.  **Confirm Understanding**: Ensure every team member explicitly reviews and signs off on their RACI assignments at program initiation.
